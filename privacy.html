Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-25

Tednically is a private, single-household set of automation tools. Its Google OAuth client ("Tednically") is used only to let the household's own Google account access its own Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts data through a command-line tool running on the household's own computers.

  • The tool is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone outside the household.
  • Google user data accessed through this client stays on the household's own devices. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
